# Content sources

What is sourced and what is editorial for each produce (issue #10). There is no review status or
approval step (spec 034): a fact either has a source or the methodology page says it is editorial.

## What is already verified

- **Nutrition values (2026-11-12):** every value equals the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy record
  cited by the produce (April 2018 release). `tests/unit/content/usda-nutrition.test.ts` checks
  this in the unit tests (CI) against `tests/fixtures/usda/sr-legacy-extract.json`.
- **Traditional uses (2026-11-12, specs 032 and 039):** the three uses that cited Wikipedia were
  removed. Dill now has one folk use from a peer-reviewed Bulgarian ethnobotanical
  study. Parsley has none: its source row also records an abortion effect, and the site cannot
  show a caution next to a use. Cabbage has none: its Bulgarian lead (a fresh-leaf compress in Kozuharova
  et al. 2013) could not be verified. New uses need a primary source (not Wikipedia).
- **Growing regions (2026-11-12, spec 033):** apples, pears and walnuts list the provinces that lead
  the МЗХ orchard structure survey (2023), with the figures and the source in each note. Cabbage
  data exists only by NUTS2 region: Plovdiv and Pazardzhik are in the South Central region, which
  has over half of the harvested open-field area (2022), but the provinces themselves are not
  ranked. Potatoes and onions follow the same NUTS2 rule with the 2025 bulletin (spec 044): the
  South-West and South-East regions lead the harvested open-field areas. The nine earlier products without a МЗХ source (quince, dill, parsley, carrots, leek, spinach, pumpkin,
  celery and beetroot)
  have no official provincial data (the surveys count them
  nationally, under „other vegetables“, or not at all, like quince), so their region notes stay
  editorial.
- **Summer vegetables (2026-11-13, spec 041):** tomatoes, peppers, aubergines and cucumbers name
  the provinces of the NUTS2 region with the most harvested open-field area in „Производство на
  зеленчуци в България – реколта 2025“ (МЗХ bulletin № 468), with the figure in each note.
  Varieties are from the ИАСАС official variety list (2026). Courgettes, green beans and okra have
  no official regional data (the survey counts them only nationally; okra under „Други
  зеленчуци“), so they list no province (`regions: []`, spec 042). Two okra varieties come from
  Български фермер, as the list has only Лясковска.
- **Summer fruits (2026-11-13, spec 040):** cherries, apricots, peaches, plums and dessert grapes
  list the provinces that lead the same МЗХ orchard survey (2023). Strawberries and melons follow
  the cabbage pattern (NUTS2, vegetables bulletin 2022). Figs have no official data: their two
  provinces quote an Agrozona interview with the Fruit-Growing Institute in Plovdiv, without
  figures. Varieties come from the ИАСАС official variety list and Български фермер articles.
- **Spring greens (2026-11-13, spec 043):** sorrel, spring onions, garlic, romaine lettuce and
  radishes have no regions: МЗХ bulletin № 468 (2025) gives green onions, garlic, lettuce and
  radishes only nationally and does not count sorrel. Their varieties come from the ИАСАС official
  variety list 2026 or Български фермер, and their growing guides from Български фермер. Nettle is
  left out: SR Legacy has only a blanched record, and EFSA's composition data lacks energy and
  macronutrients.
- **Growing guides (2026-11-12, specs 034 and 035):** every month, planting note and tip was
  checked against its cited source after the owner ruled out one gardening portal as unreliable.
  Items no source states were reworded to what the source says or removed (e.g. dill is no
  longer sown in September). New sources come from Български фермер and Agrozona.
- **Autumn staples (2026-11-13, spec 044):** potatoes, onions, broccoli, cauliflower and chestnuts
  cite USDA SR Legacy. Broccoli, cauliflower and chestnuts have no regions: МЗХ counts the first
  two only nationally and does not survey chestnuts (spec 042). Rosehip is left out: SR Legacy has
  no raw record of it, and the EFSA database gives no energy or macronutrients.
- **More fruits and nuts (2026-11-13, spec 052):** 21 entries whose months a Bulgarian source
  states (Български фермер, or the Bulgarian Wikipedia for cherry plum and hazelnut); the quotes
  are in the spec. Sour cherries, raspberries, hazelnuts and almonds name the three leading
  provinces of the МЗХ orchard survey 2023 (bulletin № 448, the confidential cells are too small to
  change the ranking); nectarines repeat the peach provinces (the survey counts them together);
  watermelons and cultivated rosehips name the South Central region (bulletins № 468 and № 469,
  2025). Twelve cite USDA SR Legacy; black currant and jujube have no fibre value there, and medlar,
  rosehip, cornelian cherry, chokeberry, cherry plum, sea buckthorn and service tree have no record,
  so they have no nutrition. Red currant and wild strawberry are left out (no source gives their
  months) and elder is excluded (toxic look-alike).
- **EFSA-sourced nutrition:** a produce that cites EFSA instead of USDA is not machine-checked; mark
  it as such in its row. Today every produce cites USDA.

### Where to look for sources

- **Seasons by month:** no official monthly calendar per produce was found (search of 2026-11-12):
  - the МЗХ „Агростатистика“ surveys give yearly areas and yields, not months;
  - the МЗХ market situation reports mention harvest timing only in passing (e.g. „Ситуация на
    пазара на пресни плодове и зеленчуци – юни 2024“: open-field cabbage harvest „в ранна фаза“ in
    June);
  - SAPI price reports are paid and not public;
  - the research institutes publish variety studies without harvest months that can be cited.

  The levels therefore stay editorial, as the methodology page says. Where a Bulgarian source
  turns up, add it to the produce or note it here.

- **Regions:** the products with МЗХ-sourced regions (see the table) follow the cited МЗХ tables. For the others, add a source
  when official provincial data appears.

## Regenerating the USDA extract

When a produce is added or its FDC record changes:

1. Download the SR Legacy CSV release:
   `https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip`
   and unzip it outside the repository.
2. Run `pnpm content:usda <unzipped-directory>`.
3. Commit the updated `tests/fixtures/usda/sr-legacy-extract.json`.
